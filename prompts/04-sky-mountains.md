# 04 · Lietajúce hory nad morom oblakov — id `sky-mountains`
Movement: `fly`. Door → `moon-sea`.

## Mood
Majestic and serene. Tall karst pillars like Zhangjiajie and Huangshan rise from a sea of clouds, and some float freely in the air (inspired by the floating-mountain feel of Avatar, look only). Waterfalls spill off floating islands into the void. Early morning golden sunrise. You glide between them like a bird.

## Scene
- **Sea of clouds** below: a soft volumetric-looking cloud layer (low-res raymarch or layered noise) that slowly flows and billows, with peaks poking through.
- **Karst pillars**: tall, vertical, eroded limestone towers with ledges, crowned with pine trees (Huangshan-style twisted pines), moss and mist wrapping their bases. Hundreds of them, fading into aerial perspective in layers.
- **Floating islands**: some pillars detach and hover with dangling roots and vines, plus long thin waterfalls that dissolve into mist before reaching the clouds.
- Birds: a few flocks gliding slowly (cranes or swifts) and a lone large bird circling.
- Floating tiny rock fragments near the big islands, drifting slowly.
- The door stands on a grassy ledge of a floating island near spawn.

## Light and color
Warm sunrise: gold, peach and rose highlights, cool blue-lavender shadows, strong aerial perspective with depth layers. Soft sun shafts through gaps.

## Sound
Wind bed with slow gusts, distant waterfalls (spatialized), occasional crane calls, a soft high pad.

## Critic poses
Wide establishing shot of layered pillars; floating island with waterfall; close pine on a ledge; looking down into the clouds; sunrise backlight through pillars; the door on its ledge.

## Avoid
Uniform repeated pillars, visible cloud slicing artifacts, a flat foggy grey look.
