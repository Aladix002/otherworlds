# 06 · Polárna žiara nad fjordom — id `aurora-fjord`
Movement: `walk`. Door → `crystal-cave`.

## Mood
A quiet Arctic winter night in a Lofoten-like fjord. Dramatic snowy peaks, a still fjord, little colorful wooden houses (red, ochre, white) with warm windows, a slowly dancing aurora overhead, and gentle snowfall. The user wants: distant forests that sway, and soft snowfall.

## Scene
- **Fjord**: dark calm water reflecting the aurora and windows, with thin ice and snow at the edges.
- **Mountains**: steep jagged peaks with snow and rock, layered into distance.
- **Village**: 10–25 small wooden houses on stilts by the water (rorbuer), drying racks, a small pier, boats, warm window glow, chimney smoke drifting.
- **Forest**: snowy pine and spruce forests on slopes that **sway gently in the wind**, with snow occasionally sliding off branches.
- **Aurora**: curtain aurora, green dominant with purple and pink lower edges, slowly rippling and folding (multiple layered ribbons), and occasional gentle brightening pulses.
- **Snowfall**: soft flakes, varying density over minutes, never a blizzard. Your footsteps leave soft prints and sound.
- Stars behind the aurora.

## Light and color
Blue snow light, green-violet aurora reflected on snow and water, warm amber windows.

## Sound
Soft wind, distant water lapping on the pier, snow-crunch footsteps, a very soft aurora "hum" pad, occasional creak of wood or a far dog bark (very rare, distant).

## Critic poses
Village with aurora reflected in the fjord; peaks with aurora curtains; forest slope swaying; close house with warm window and snow; looking up into the aurora; the door on the snowy shore.

## Avoid
Aurora that looks like a static texture, flicker, overexposed green, snow shimmer.
