# 10 · Púšť snov — id `dream-desert`
Movement: `walk`. Door → `light-library`.

## Mood
A surreal, colorful, non-realistic desert. It begins in Antelope-Canyon-like slot canyons where beams of light fall from above and dust dances in them in different shapes (spirals, rings, small swirls). The canyon opens onto vast dunes under the Milky Way, where psychedelic glowing lizards and snakes wander. Magical, warm, mysterious. The user explicitly wants it colorful, not necessarily realistic.

## Scene
- **Slot canyon (spawn)**: flowing, wave-carved sandstone walls (SDF, layered strata) in orange, pink, purple and magenta gradients, narrow and winding. Light beams fall from slits above, and **dust particles in the beams form slow dancing shapes**: spirals, rings, small vortices that dissolve and reform.
- **Canyon exit** onto the dunes: a time-of-day shift (dusk → night) along the path; outside it is night.
- **Dunes**: vast smooth dunes with wind ripples, sand blowing gently off crests, under a dense Milky Way with saturated colors.
- **Glowing creatures**: lizards and snakes with bioluminescent patterns (neon lines, rainbow scales) slowly wandering, leaving faint glowing tracks in the sand that fade. Few, gentle, never approaching aggressively.
- A few strange desert plants with glowing tips and a lone rock arch.

## Light and color
Canyon: hot orange-magenta bounce light and saturated beams. Night: deep purple-blue sky, star colors, neon creature glow. Dithering on the night sky.

## Sound
Canyon: soft wind whistle and resonant tones. Dunes: gentle sand hiss, a warm low drone, sparse plucked or harp-like notes, faint creature chirps.

## Critic poses
Canyon with light beam and dancing dust; canyon walls close (strata detail); canyon exit view onto dunes; dunes under the Milky Way; a glowing lizard with tracks; the door on a dune.

## Avoid
Flat untextured sand, too-real dullness, beam banding, dust that looks like noise.
