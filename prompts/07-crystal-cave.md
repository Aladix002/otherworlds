# 07 · Kryštálová jaskyňa — id `crystal-cave`
Movement: `walk` (with gentle climbing via smooth step-up on rock). Door → `spirit-forest`.

## Mood
A vast underground hall of giant crystals, like Naica's Cave of Crystals blended with amethyst geodes and a dark-fantasy crystal cavern. Crystals glow softly from within, and light refracts into colors. Mysterious, sacred and quiet.

## Scene
- **Hall**: large irregular cavern (SDF-based), with several connected chambers and openings, plus a shallow underground pool that reflects everything.
- **Giant selenite beams**: huge translucent milky-white crystal beams (meters thick, tens of meters long) crossing the space at angles.
- **Amethyst geode walls**: clusters of violet crystal points lining walls and alcoves, with sparkly facets.
- **Inner glow**: crystals emit soft light that pulses very slowly, and nearby crystals brighten as you approach. Colored refraction caustics on surfaces (fake dispersion).
- Floating dust and mote particles catch the light. Occasional drops fall from the ceiling into the pool, with ripples.
- A distant opening with a shaft of cold daylight or moonlight for contrast.

## Light and color
Dark rock with cool white, violet and soft cyan crystal light, and rainbow dispersion accents. Strong but soft bloom.

## Sound
Deep resonant cave drone, crystal "singing" tones (harmonic, bowl-like) triggered softly near crystals, sparse drips with long reverb.

## Critic poses
Hall with giant beams; amethyst geode alcove close-up; pool reflection; dispersion caustics on rock; shaft of light; the door in a chamber.

## Avoid
Plastic-looking crystals, aliasing on thin crystal edges, too-dark crushed blacks, noisy refraction.
