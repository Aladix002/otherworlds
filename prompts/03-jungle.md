# 03 · Bioluminiscenčná džungľa s leknami — id `jungle`
Movement: `walk` (with gentle wading in shallow water). Door → `sky-mountains`.

## Mood
A psychedelic, bioluminescent rainforest at night, inspired by the feeling of Avatar's Pandora (look and feel only: no characters, creatures or designs from the film). Colors gently pulse and flow, as if the forest breathes. At its heart is a waterfall pouring into a lagoon full of giant lily pads and lotuses that float, slowly open and close, with fish and frogs.

## Scene
- Dense jungle around a clearing: giant twisted trees with buttress roots, hanging vines, huge fern fronds, fractal, spiral and fan-shaped plants. Many are emissive with veins of cyan, magenta, violet and lime that **pulse in slow traveling waves**, and the waves flow across the forest.
- **Touch response**: plants near the player brighten softly and curl or unfurl as you pass.
- **Waterfall and lagoon**: a waterfall falls from a cliff into a calm lagoon, with mist and glowing spray. On the water float **giant Victoria-style lily pads** (some you can walk across) and **lotus flowers** that slowly bloom and close on long cycles, plus drifting petals. Glowing koi-like fish swim under the surface. Small frogs sit on pads and occasionally hop (with a soft "plip" and ripples).
- Floating seeds or spores of light drift upward, and fireflies fly slowly.
- A layer of mist near the ground and the water, with soft light shafts from a hidden moon.

## Light and color
Night with a deep teal-violet ambient. Emissive plants are the main light. Saturated but harmonious: no rainbow vomit; keep 3–4 dominant hues that slowly rotate over minutes. Bloom glow on emissives.

## Sound
Night jungle bed of soft insects and frogs (synthesized), the waterfall's distant noise rising as you approach, slow pads that follow the pulse cycle, and occasional magical chimes when a lotus opens.

## Critic poses
Waterfall and lagoon wide shot; lily pads and lotuses close-up; a glowing forest path; looking up into the canopy with drifting spores; pulse waves across the plants; the door at the edge of the clearing.

## Avoid
Strobing or fast color changes, a chaotic over-saturated mess, flat glowing cards, any recognizable copyrighted design.
