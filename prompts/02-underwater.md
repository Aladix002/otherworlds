# 02 · Podmorský svet — od tyrkysu po hlbinu — id `underwater`
Movement: `swim`. Doors: → `jungle` (place it in the shallow turquoise temple area).

## Mood
One continuous dive from bright, joyful shallows to a calm, mysterious deep. Gentle living things swim by peacefully, never crowded. The user asked for a turquoise, ancient Greek sunken temple, golden kelp forests with light beams, and calm life.

## Scene (three zones connected by gentle slopes; the player chooses how deep to go)
1. **Turquoise shallows (spawn, ~5–15 m deep)**: white sand ripples, very clear turquoise water, a wavy surface overhead with caustics everywhere, and a **sunken ancient Greek temple**: fluted marble columns (some standing, some fallen), a pediment, steps, broken statues and amphorae, with patches of seagrass and small anemones. Sea turtles glide, small colorful fish dart lazily, and an occasional ray settles on the sand.
2. **Golden kelp forest (~15–40 m)**: towering kelp columns swaying, golden-green light, god rays, rocks with sponges and coral, fish schools (boids), a pair of manta rays looping slowly.
3. **The deep (~40–120 m)**: light fades to deep blue then near black. Bioluminescence: siphonophore chains, comb jellies with rainbow cilia, small glowing particles, a distant slow silhouette of a large animal (whale shark or whale) passing far away. Calm, mysterious, never scary.
- Depth drives fog color, light absorption (red disappears first), caustics fading and exposure, all continuous.

## Light and color
Shallows: turquoise, white, warm sun. Kelp: gold and green. Deep: ultramarine to black with cyan, violet and green glows. Caustics and god rays are central to the look. Use dithering, because deep gradients band easily.

## Sound
Shallows: bright water bed, soft bubbles, distant surf. Kelp: muffled current, creaks, a soft whale call. Deep: low pressure drone, sparse glassy bioluminescent "pings". Crossfade by depth.

## Critic poses
Temple columns in turquoise light with fish; looking up at the surface through columns; kelp forest with god rays; mantas; transition slope from kelp into the deep; the deep with siphonophores and comb jellies.

## Avoid
Uniform fog soup, muddy greens, popping kelp LODs, shimmering caustics aliasing, fish clipping through geometry, and copying Abyssal Garden's layout or look one-to-one (it is inspiration only).
