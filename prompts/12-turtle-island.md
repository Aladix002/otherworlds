# 12 · Korytnačí ostrov — id `turtle-island`
Movement: `walk` on the island; the ocean around is a soft boundary. Door → `astral-ocean` (closes the ring).

## Mood
You stand on a volcanic island at calm dusk, and slowly realize the island is on the back of a colossal sea turtle slowly swimming across the ocean. The turtle's head rises from the sea ahead now and then, and its giant flippers stroke slowly at the island's edges. Black sand beaches, a gently smoking little volcano with glowing lava streams trickling into the sea (steam), lush green slopes. Calm and majestic.

## Scene
- **Island**: a volcanic cone with green slopes (like Guishan Island, Taiwan), cliffs, black sand beaches (Reynisfjara-like basalt columns at one end), palms and ferns.
- **Volcano**: gentle steam and smoke plume and a few slow glowing lava rivulets running down to the shore, steaming where they meet the sea. Peaceful, not explosive.
- **The turtle**: the island's base is a giant shell (hexagonal scute patterns visible at the shore and cliffs). Huge flippers emerge from the sea at the sides, moving in slow strokes that create big gentle waves. The head occasionally rises in the distance ahead, breathes (a mist puff) and sinks. The whole island moves slowly, so the ocean flows past (wake, foam lines).
- **Ocean**: dusk-colored, gently rolling, with foam and a wake trailing behind.
- **Sky**: dusk gradient, the first stars, a big soft sun just set, clouds lit pink and orange.
- Seabirds circling the island.

## Light and color
Warm dusk: orange, pink and violet, with black sand, green slopes and glowing orange lava accents.

## Sound
Ocean waves and wake, a deep slow turtle breath (very low, rare), wind, distant seabirds, soft lava crackle near flows, a warm pad.

## Critic poses
Island with turtle head rising in the distance; flipper stroke with waves; lava rivulet meeting the sea in steam; black sand beach with basalt columns; the volcano summit view; the door on the beach.

## Avoid
The island looking static (the motion must read), lava looking dangerous, scale confusion.
