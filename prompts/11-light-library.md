# 11 · Svätyňa svetla a kníh — id `light-library`
Movement: `walk` (with a few gently floating "lift" platforms or spiral stairs to reach upper galleries; or allow `fly` inside — choose what feels best).

Door → `turtle-island`.

## Mood
An endless, impossible library-sanctuary: baroque white-and-gold halls (Admont Abbey, Trinity College Long Room) whose tall windows are colored glass like the Nasir al-Mulk mosque, painting the floor with colored light. Dust drifts in the beams. Through the windows: stars and a galaxy instead of a landscape. Books occasionally float off shelves, turn pages slowly and drift back. Silent, sacred, wondrous.

## Scene
- **Halls**: long barrel-vaulted halls with painted ceilings (procedural soft cloudscape frescos), galleries on two or three levels, wooden shelves full of procedurally varied books (spine colors and heights), statues, globes, reading desks with open books.
- **Endlessness**: halls connect into a branching or looping structure that feels infinite (repeat with variation; distant halls fade into golden haze).
- **Stained glass**: tall windows of geometric Persian-style colored glass. Sunlight passes through, projecting colored patterns onto floor and shelves that move slowly as the "sun" drifts.
- **Beyond the windows**: deep space with a galaxy and nebula.
- **Floating books**: a few at a time lift out, open, flutter pages and glide back. Small glowing letters or motes sometimes rise from open pages and dissolve.
- Dust motes in all beams.

## Light and color
Warm white, cream and gold interior, jewel-colored light patches, cool space blue through windows. Bloom on beams.

## Sound
Large-hall reverb, a very soft choir-like pad (formant synthesis), page-turn rustles, distant soft bells, footsteps on wood and stone.

## Critic poses
Long hall perspective; stained-glass light pattern on the floor; galaxy through a window; a floating book with glowing letters; upper gallery looking down; the door between shelves.

## Avoid
Repetition that looks like copy-paste, aliasing on book spines and railings, flat lighting.
