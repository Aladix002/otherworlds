# 08 · Duchovný les svetlušiek — id `spirit-forest`
Movement: `walk`. Door → `meadow`.

## Mood
An ancient mossy laurel cloud forest at night (Madeira's Fanal, Yakushima). Gnarled trees are thick with moss, and fog drifts at ground level. Fireflies are everywhere but gentle, mushrooms glow, and weeping willows have glowing hanging strands. In the distance, friendly spirit lights and quiet forest beings: slightly spooky, but warm, kind and pleasant. The user loves the idea of the beings.

## Scene
- **Trees**: twisted, wide, gnarled laurel trees covered in moss and ferns, with roots over mossy stones. Paths meander naturally.
- **Glowing willows**: a few large weeping willows whose hanging strands glow softly and sway.
- **Bioluminescent fungi**: clusters of green-glowing mycena-style mushrooms on logs and roots, and shelf fungi.
- **Fireflies**: thousands of gentle blinking lights at varied heights with asynchronous, organic blinking (occasional slow synchronized waves).
- **Spirit lights**: soft wisps that hover in the distance, drift away if you approach, and sometimes lead along a path.
- **Forest beings**: a few tall, calm, abstract silhouettes (e.g. a gentle deer-like spirit with glowing antlers or lantern-like small creatures). They are friendly and slow. They notice you, look, then wander on. They are original designs, not from any film.
- **Fog**: low ground fog layers with light shafts from a hidden moon.

## Light and color
Deep green-blue night, warm firefly yellow-green, mint fungi glow, pale spirit light. Contrast between darkness and soft points of light.

## Sound
Night forest bed (soft crickets, leaf rustle), gentle wind in trees, a soft airy pad, rare soft "spirit" chimes, a distant owl.

## Critic poses
Mossy path with fireflies and fog; glowing willow; fungi on a log close-up; distant spirit lights between trees; a forest being looking at the camera; the door in a small clearing.

## Avoid
Horror vibes, sudden appearances, too-dark scenes, firefly flicker that reads as noise.
