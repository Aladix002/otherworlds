# 05 · Mesačné zrkadlové more — id `moon-sea`
Movement: `walk` (on mirror-like shallow water a few cm deep, so you walk on the sky). Door → `aurora-fjord`.

## Mood
Utter stillness and wonder. A shallow, endless mirror sea under a huge moon that fills a big part of the sky. Stars above and below. At a distant shore, waves light up blue with bioluminescence. Warm paper lanterns slowly rise from the water into the sky.

## Scene
- **Mirror water**: near-perfect reflection (screen-space or planar reflection; the planar re-render of sky and lanterns is cheap), with gentle rings where you step and very slow, faint ripples from a breeze.
- **Moon**: huge, detailed (procedural maria and craters), soft halo, a moon-glitter path on distant deeper water.
- **Sky**: Milky Way band, dense stars, faint airglow at the horizon, an occasional slow meteor.
- **Bioluminescent shore**: a low beach and dunes at a distance where gentle waves break and glow cyan. Walking there, footsteps in the wet sand sparkle.
- **Lanterns**: dozens to hundreds of warm lanterns slowly lifting off the water and drifting up with gentle sway and flicker. Keep them spaced out: a slow stream, not a swarm.
- A few small rocks and a lone small tree on a sandbar to give scale.

## Light and color
Cool silver-blue moonlight versus warm lantern orange, and cyan bioluminescence. Deep night sky, but readable. Dithering is essential.

## Sound
Near silence: a soft water bed, delicate splashes on steps, distant soft surf from the shore, a warm low pad, and rare glassy tones as lanterns rise.

## Critic poses
Mirror horizon with moon; lanterns rising with reflections; glowing shore waves; looking straight down at reflected stars; the lone tree; the door standing in the water.

## Avoid
Reflection seams, star shimmer or aliasing, lantern overcrowding, harsh moon bloom.
